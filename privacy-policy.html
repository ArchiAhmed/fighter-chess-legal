Fighter Chess is a chess game in which every piece is an aircraft. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • The campaign, challenges and local multiplayer work offline, and what you do there stays on your device.
  • When the game connects to our game server, it makes an online account for you automatically (see below). The server keeps a callsign, an account ID, your Google Play Games player ID (if you sign in with it), your online results, friends and purchases.
  • We do not show ads, do not use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and do not sell or share your information with anyone for advertising.
  • You can delete your account at any time from inside the game.

Information kept on your device

Your squadron name, emblem and fleet choices, campaign progress, settings, and a cache of your account's balance and items. This stays in the app's storage on your device. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data. If Android backup is on, Android may include it in your device backup (handled by Google under your Google account settings).

Your online account

When the game first reaches our game server (in the background, or when you open ONLINE), it signs you in with Google Play Games if you use it, or otherwise makes a guest account. A guest account's callsign is made from your squadron name; a Google Play Games account's callsign is made from your Play Games name. Callsigns are public. A guest account that later signs in with Google Play Games keeps its progress.

Google

The game uses Google Play services:

  • Google Play Games Services for sign-in. Google tells our server your Play Games player ID and display name. Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing for purchases. Google handles the payment. When you buy, the game gives Google Play two scrambled references, one to your Fighter Chess account and one to your Google Play Games player (never your callsign, ID or player ID themselves), which Google stores with the purchase. Our server asks Google Play to confirm each purchase and to return those stored references, and compares them with your account's before giving or restoring anything. This checks the references attached to the purchase; it is not a separate check of your Google identity.

We receive from Google only what is described above: never your email address, contacts or payment details.

Points and fair play

Points for matches against the computer are only given after our server replays the match. Records of matches, reports and flags of unusual activity (such as very frequent rematches) are kept so a person can review possible cheating. Nobody is penalised automatically.

Why we use it (legal bases)

  • To provide the game you asked for (performance of our contract with you): your account, online play, friends, chat, points, purchases and restoring them.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping play fair and safe (checking matches, rate limits, reports, flags, blocking) and protecting the service from abuse.
  • Legal obligations: keeping purchase records for tax and accounting.

Who can see what

  • Other players see your callsign, account ID, squadron name, emblem, fleet look, online rating, rank and online results, and what you write in chat.
  • We (the developer) can see what the server keeps, to run the game and handle support, reports and refunds.
  • Service providers that run the game for us: Google (sign-in and payments) and the hosting provider of our game server, which stores the server's data on our behalf. The game server has not been launched yet. Before it starts collecting any information, this policy will be updated to name the hosting provider and the country where the data is stored. Your information may then be processed outside your own country, with the safeguards the law requires.
  • We do not sell your information or share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long it is kept

  • Your account is kept until you delete it. A guest account (one never signed in with Google Play Games) that is not used for 12 months is deleted automatically, because without its device nobody can prove it is theirs or use it again.
  • Matches against the computer that you hand in are kept with your account, to show your verified record and prevent the same match being paid twice.
  • When you delete your account, your callsign, profile, friends, blocks, Google Play Games link, sign-ins, tickets, match submissions, items bought with points and chat messages are removed straight away. Purchase records and the points history are kept, attached only to an anonymous account number, only for as long as we need them to meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations and to handle refunds, chargebacks and disputes, and are then deleted. If a pack from a deleted account is later restored to your new account, its purchase record moves to the new account and notes the anonymous number it came from. Past online match results stay in the rankings history under the anonymous account number. Reports and flags already made about the account (including its callsign and the reported chat) are kept for safety review; only the most recent 1,000 review items are kept.
  • Backups: once the game server runs, it is set up to keep backup copies for no more than 14 days (including any copies stored elsewhere), so deleted information leaves the backups within 14 days.

Deleting your account

You can delete your online account in either of these ways. Each one checks that the account is yours: we never delete an account because someone gives its callsign, pilot ID, squadron name or match history, since other players can see those.

  • In the game: ONLINE, then DELETE ACCOUNT under your callsign, and confirm twice. This works on any device where you are signed in to the account.
  • On the web, without the game: a deletion page on our game server lets you sign in with the Google account you play with. Google tells us which Google Play Games player you are, and only the account linked to that player is shown and can be deleted. Its address is given at https://archiahmed.github.io/fighter-chess-legal/delete-account.html once the game server is launched.

Guest accounts (never signed in with Google Play Games) can only be deleted in the game on the device they were made on, because only that device holds their sign-in. If that device or the game's data is gone, nobody can use the account again, and it is deleted automatically after 12 months without use. Purchases need a Google Play Games account, so guest accounts never hold purchases.

If you need help, email ag.gaming.dev@gmail.com. We will reply with how to delete your account in one of the ways above; we cannot delete an account on the strength of an email alone, because we do not store email addresses.

Paid packs and points: packs you buy belong to you as the Google Play Games player who bought them. If you delete your account and later sign in again with the same Google account, RESTORE PURCHASES gives packs that are still valid back to your new account. Before doing so, our server asks Google Play to confirm that each purchase is still paid for (not refunded, cancelled or charged back), and checks that the player reference stored with the purchase, which the game attached when you bought it, matches your new account's Google Play Games player. Packs are never moved away from an account that still exists, and never to another player. Packs whose purchase carries no player reference (for example, a promo code redeemed in the Play Store) cannot be restored after deletion. Points, including bought points, are used up when they are added to your balance, so they are removed with the account and are not restored; refunds of Google Play purchases follow Google Play's refund policy. If you want a refund, ask before deleting your account.

Your campaign progress on your device is not affected; uninstall the game or clear its data to remove it.

Security

The game talks to the server only over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in tokens are stored only as hashes, purchases are checked with Google, and access to the server is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

Fighter Chess is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.
