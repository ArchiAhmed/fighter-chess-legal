Status: the Fighter Chess game server has not been launched yet. Until it is, no account information is kept on any server, so there is nothing to delete there. This page describes how deletion works once it is launched.

Each way of deleting below checks that the account is yours. We never delete an account because someone gives its callsign, pilot ID, squadron name or match history, since other players can see those.

In the game

  1. Open Fighter Chess and tap ONLINE.
  2. Under your callsign, tap DELETE ACCOUNT.
  3. Confirm twice.

This works on any device where you are signed in to the account. The account is deleted straight away and signed out on every device.

On the web, without the game

For accounts signed in with Google Play Games: once the game server is launched, its deletion page will be linked here. There you sign in with the Google account you play with; Google tells us which Google Play Games player you are, and only the account linked to that player is shown and can be deleted.

Guest accounts

Guest accounts (never signed in with Google Play Games) can only be deleted in the game on the device they were made on, because only that device holds their sign-in. If that device or the game's data is gone, nobody can use the account again, and it is deleted automatically after 12 months without use. Purchases need a Google Play Games account, so guest accounts never hold purchases.

Help

Email ag.gaming.dev@gmail.com. We will reply with how to delete your account in one of the ways above; we cannot delete an account on the strength of an email alone, because we do not store email addresses.

What is deleted

  • Your callsign, squadron name, emblem and fleet look
  • Your Google Play Games link and every sign-in
  • Your friends, friend requests and blocked players
  • Your chat messages, match tickets and the matches against the computer you handed in
  • Your points balance and items bought with points
  • Paid packs, until you restore them to a new account (see below)

Paid packs and points

Packs you buy belong to you as the Google Play Games player who bought them. If you delete your account and later sign in again with the same Google account, RESTORE PURCHASES gives packs that are still valid back to your new account. Before doing so, our server asks Google Play to confirm that each purchase is still paid for (not refunded, cancelled or charged back), and checks that the player reference stored with the purchase, which the game attached when you bought it, matches your new account's Google Play Games player. This checks the reference attached to the purchase; it is not a separate check of your Google identity. Packs are never moved away from an account that still exists, and never to another player. Packs whose purchase carries no player reference (for example, a promo code redeemed in the Play Store) cannot be restored after deletion. Points, including bought points, are used up when they are added to your balance, so they are removed with the account and are not restored; refunds of Google Play purchases follow Google Play's refund policy. If you want a refund, ask before deleting your account.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records (product, order number, time) and the points history are kept, attached only to an anonymous account number, only for as long as needed to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations and to handle refunds, chargebacks and disputes, and are then deleted. If a pack is later restored to your new account, its purchase record moves with it and notes the anonymous number it came from.
  • Past online match results stay in the rankings history under the anonymous account number.
  • Reports and flags already made about the account, with the reported chat, are kept for safety review (only the most recent 1,000 review items are kept).
  • Backups: once the game server runs, it is set up to keep backup copies for no more than 14 days, so deleted information leaves the backups within 14 days.

Your campaign progress stays on your own device until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
